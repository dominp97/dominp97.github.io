FitWard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Domingo Peiro ("we", "FitWard"), reachable at appscalatayud@gmail.com. This policy explains what personal data FitWard (package com.dominpl.fitward) processes, why, on what legal basis, who receives it and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

2. Data we process

Account data. Name and email of your Google account, or an anonymous identifier if you use a guest account, plus an internal user ID.

Profile data you provide. Goal, height, sex, preferred workout types and difficulty, available equipment, body zones, weekly goal and reminder time. Providing height and sex is optional and only used to personalize your routine.

Progress data. Completed days, current and best streak, total days, medals unlocked, the exercises of each daily challenge and completion timestamps.

Social data (optional). If you use Friends, your display name, your friend code and your current streak are visible to users who add you, and you can see theirs. You can remove a friend at any time.

Technical and diagnostic data. If the app crashes, an anonymous report (device model, Android version, app version, stack trace) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. We also collect anonymous usage events (for example "workout completed", screens viewed) with Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used.

Advertising data. FitWard shows ads served by Google AdMob. For this, Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device information and ad interaction data.

Data stored on your device. A local copy of your progress and the exercise catalog so the app works offline, your preferences, and the reminder schedule.

Apart from advertising data, we do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and we do not read health data from Health Connect or Apple Health.

Height, sex and workout data may be considered health-related. We process them only to personalize your routine, on the basis of your explicit consent when you enter them (Art. 9.2.a). You can remove or change them at any time.

We do not sell your data and we do not make decisions with legal effects based on automated profiling. The daily challenge is selected by a deterministic algorithm from your preferences. Ads may be personalized only if you consent; otherwise you will see non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Who receives your data

  • Appwrite (backend hosting, authentication and database) acts as our processor. Each user can only read and write their own data.
  • Google provides sign-in (Google Sign-In) and Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) as processors.
  • Other users you add as friends see the limited data described in section 2.
  • Authorities, when legally required.

Google AdMob (advertising) receives the advertising data described above to serve and measure ads, acting as an independent controller under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell your data or share your profile, progress or health-related data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. International transfers

Some providers (for example Google/Firebase) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses, and the providers' own safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and progress: while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we delete them from our server.
  • Crash and analytics data: retained by Firebase for the periods set in its console (typically up to 14 months for analytics).
  • Backups may keep residual copies for a limited time before being overwritten.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the database is protected by your session and per-user permissions. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent without affecting past processing.

In the app: view and edit your profile in Settings, export your data and delete your account and all data in Settings → Account and privacy. Otherwise write to appscalatayud@gmail.com; we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Spain: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

9. Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. It is not sent from our servers. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android's notification permissions.

10. Minors

FitWard is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 14 to 17 should use the app with the knowledge of their parents or guardians.

11. Third-party links

The app may link to external pages (for example this policy or the dataset credits). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are material, we will tell you in the app and show the new date at the top. Continued use after that means you have been informed.
